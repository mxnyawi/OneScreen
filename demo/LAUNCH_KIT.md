# OneScreen launch kit

Copy for posting the videos. Draft, so edit to taste. Don't post until the repo is public and the
README has the gesture GIF at the top.

## Hooks (first line / on-screen text)

Use one per platform. Don't post the same text everywhere.

- "POV: your monitor is right there and your mouse says nope."
- "I moved my laptop to the other side of my monitor. My Mac didn't notice."
- "Stop dragging rectangles in Display Settings."
- "Your mouse hits an invisible wall every time you change desks. Here's the fix."

## X / Twitter (16:9 or 1:1 video)

> Every time I move my laptop to the other side of my monitor, the mouse hits an invisible wall.
>
> So I made OneScreen: just push the cursor toward your monitor and it flips the display layout
> for you. No settings, no clicks.
>
> Free + open source, macOS & Windows 👇
> github.com/mxnyawi/OneScreen

## TikTok / Reels / Shorts (9:16)

- Caption: `the display settings dance is over #productivity #macsetup #desksetup #coding #wfh`
- Render with `music: false` and add a trending sound in the app.
- Pin a comment with the GitHub link.

## Hacker News

- Title: `Show HN: OneScreen – push your cursor toward a monitor to fix the display layout`
- Link to the repo, not the video. Put the wide gesture GIF at the top of the README.
- First comment: why it exists, how detection works (raw mouse movement at a screen edge with no
  display beyond it, threshold of about 400 px in 1.2 s), and what it doesn't do (no camera, no
  sensors, no network traffic).

## Reddit (r/macapps, r/Windows11, r/battlestations, r/productivity)

Use the 1:1 video. Title example: `I got tired of my mouse hitting a wall every time I switched
desks, so I made a free app that fixes the layout when you push toward the monitor`.
